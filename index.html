Amity Coding Club is the official student-led developer collective at Amity University Madhya Pradesh. Born from the belief that students should never be left behind in a fast-evolving tech world, we empower builders to move past theory, write production code, and turn ideas into impactful real-world solutions.

Community First

Connecting 140+ engineers & designers through shared knowledge, code reviews, and late-night sprints.

Hands-On Innovation

Hackathons, prototyping sprints, and systems workshops that end in shipped production repos.

Ideas Over Hierarchy

First-years lead projects just as often as seniors. If your code compiles and solves the problem, it ships.
